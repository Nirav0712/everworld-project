What is a Tourist Visa?
A tourist visa allows temporary travel abroad for sightseeing, holidays, and recreational activities only.
How long can I stay on a tourist visa?
The duration depends on the country and visa type, usually ranging from a few weeks to several months.
Can I work on a tourist visa?
No. Tourist visas strictly prohibit employment or business activities.
What if my tourist visa is rejected?
Our experts analyze rejection reasons and help reapply with improved documentation and strategy.
