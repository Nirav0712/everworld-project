Tourist Visas to Canada

Canada is a world-class tourism destination known for its natural beauty, vibrant cities, cultural diversity, and iconic landmarks. From the stunning Niagara Falls and Rocky Mountains to modern cities like Toronto, Vancouver, and Montreal, Canada offers unforgettable travel experiences. A Canada Tourist Visa (Temporary Resident Visa – TRV) allows you to visit Canada for sightseeing, holidays, leisure travel, and short-term tourism activities.

Tourist Visas Documents
  • Valid Passport (minimum 6 months validity)
  • Tourist Visa Application Form
  • Purpose of Travel / Tourism Cover Letter
  • Travel Itinerary & Hotel Booking
  • Proof of Funds & Bank Statements
  • Employment / Business Proof

Education Visas to Canada

Canada is one of the top destinations for international students, known for its globally recognized education system, affordable tuition fees, and excellent post-study work opportunities. A Canada Education Visa (Study Permit) allows students to study at designated learning institutions (DLIs) and build a strong academic and professional future. At Ever World Immigration, we provide expert Canada study visa services, guiding students from course selection to visa approval with complete transparency and professional support.

Education Visas Documents
  • Letter of Acceptance (LOA) from a Canadian DLI
  • Academic Certificates & Transcripts
  • Proof of Funds & Financial Documents
  • Statement of Purpose (SOP)
  • IELTS / English Language Test (if required)
  • Biometrics & Visa Application Form
