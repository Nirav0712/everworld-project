How can we help you

Ask Your Immigration Questions &
Get Expert Guidance

What are the chances of student visa approval?
Visa approval depends on your academic profile, financial strength, course relevance, and documentation. Our experts help you build a strong profile to maximize success.
Can I work while studying abroad?
Yes, most countries allow international students to work part-time during studies and full-time during holidays.
Do you help with university selection?
Absolutely. We guide you in selecting the right country, university, and course based on your profile and career goals.
What if my visa gets rejected?
We analyze rejection reasons and re-apply with a stronger strategy and improved documentation.
Do you assist with the complete application process?
Yes. From profile evaluation, university applications, documentation, offer letters, visa filing, and interview preparation—we provide end-to-end support to ensure a smooth and stress-free journey.
What is a PR visa?
A PR visa allows you to permanently live, work, and settle in a foreign country with legal residency rights.
Which country is best for PR?
The best country depends on your age, education, work experience, and language scores. We help you choose the most suitable option.
How long does PR processing take?
Processing time varies by country and program. Our experts ensure faster processing with correct documentation.
Can I include my family in PR?
Yes, PR applications usually allow you to include your spouse and dependent children.
Do I need a job offer to apply for PR?
Not always. Many PR programs allow you to apply without a job offer based on your skills, qualifications, and points score. Our experts guide you on the best pathway for your profile.
What is a Visitor Visa?
A visitor visa allows temporary stay in a foreign country for non-immigration purposes like tourism, family visits, or business meetings.
How long can I stay on a visitor visa?
The duration depends on the country and visa type, typically ranging from a few weeks to several months.
Is an invitation letter mandatory?
Not always. It depends on the purpose of visit and country requirements. Our team will guide you accordingly.
What if my visitor visa is rejected?
We analyze rejection reasons and re-apply with a stronger case and improved documentation.
Can a visitor visa be converted into a work or PR visa?
Generally, visitor visas cannot be converted. However, in some cases and countries, options may be available. Our experts guide you on the correct and legal pathway based on your situation.
What is IELTS?
IELTS (International English Language Testing System) is a globally recognized English proficiency test required for education, work, and immigration.
What band score do I need?
Required band score depends on country, visa type, and institution. Our trainers guide you based on your goal.
Do you provide mock tests?
Yes, we conduct regular full-length mock tests with detailed performance evaluation.
Is IELTS mandatory for PR and study?
In most countries, IELTS is mandatory for student visas, PR, and work permits.
Can I prepare for IELTS without prior English training?
Yes. With the right guidance and structured practice, even beginners can achieve a good band score. Our coaching focuses on step-by-step improvement in all four modules.
What is a Work Permit visa?
A work permit visa allows foreign nationals to work legally in another country for a specific period and job role.
Do I need a job offer for a work permit?
In most cases, yes. Some countries offer open work permits depending on eligibility. Our experts guide you accordingly.
How long does work permit processing take?
Processing time depends on the country and visa category. We ensure timely submission to avoid delays.
Can a work permit lead to PR?
Yes, many countries provide PR pathways after gaining work experience. We help plan your long-term settlement strategy.
Can I change my employer on a work permit?
It depends on the country and type of work permit. Some permits are employer-specific, while others allow flexibility. Our experts guide you through the correct process to stay compliant with visa rules.
What is PTE?
PTE (Pearson Test of English) is a computer-based English proficiency test accepted worldwide for study, work, and immigration.
Is PTE accepted for PR and student visas?
Yes, PTE is accepted by many countries including Australia, Canada, UK, and New Zealand.
How is PTE different from IELTS?
PTE is fully computer-based with AI scoring, faster results, and no human examiner for speaking.
Do you provide mock tests?
Yes, we conduct regular full-length PTE mock tests with detailed score analysis.
How do PTE mock tests help improve my score?
PTE mock tests help you understand the exam pattern, manage time effectively, identify weak areas, and improve accuracy with detailed feedback and performance analysis.
What is a Tourist Visa?
A tourist visa allows temporary travel abroad for sightseeing, holidays, and recreational activities only.
How long can I stay on a tourist visa?
The duration depends on the country and visa type, usually ranging from a few weeks to several months.
Can I work on a tourist visa?
No. Tourist visas strictly prohibit employment or business activities.
What if my tourist visa is rejected?
Our experts analyze rejection reasons and help reapply with improved documentation and strategy.
Is travel insurance mandatory for a tourist visa?
Many countries require valid travel insurance covering medical emergencies and trip duration. Our team guides you in choosing the right insurance as per visa requirements.
What is TOEFL?
TOEFL (Test of English as a Foreign Language) is an internationally accepted English proficiency test used for study, work, and immigration purposes.
Is TOEFL accepted for PR and student visas?
Yes, TOEFL is widely accepted by universities and immigration authorities in countries like the USA, Canada, UK, Australia, and New Zealand.
How is TOEFL different from IELTS?
TOEFL is primarily internet-based (iBT) and focuses more on academic English, with integrated tasks combining reading, listening, speaking, and writing.
Do you provide mock tests?
Yes, we conduct regular full-length TOEFL mock tests with detailed performance and score analysis.
Do you offer complete TOEFL coaching support?
Absolutely. Our coaching includes expert guidance, structured study plans, practice materials, speaking evaluations, and exam strategies to help you achieve your target score.
