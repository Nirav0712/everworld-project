What is a Work Permit visa?
A work permit visa allows foreign nationals to work legally in another country for a specific period and job role.
Do I need a job offer for a work permit?
In most cases, yes. Some countries offer open work permits depending on eligibility. Our experts guide you accordingly.
How long does work permit processing take?
Processing time depends on the country and visa category. We ensure timely submission to avoid delays.
Can a work permit lead to PR?
Yes, many countries provide PR pathways after gaining work experience. We help plan your long-term settlement strategy.
